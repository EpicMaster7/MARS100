**Field:** Aerospace engineering / space mission architecture

**Goal:** Compare three conceptual architectures for delivering at least 100 metric tons of useful cargo to the Martian surface: four direct 25t cargo landers, one 100t spacecraft with Earth-orbit refueling, and a redundant multi-lander predeployment campaign.

**Success metric:** Build a quantitative model using idealized orbital mechanics, rocket-equation propellant sizing, cost estimates, and assumed mission risks; identify the lowest-cost architecture that reaches at least a 95% modeled probability of delivering the full cargo target. Present visuals and a reproducible model, clearly explaining the assumptions and limitations.
